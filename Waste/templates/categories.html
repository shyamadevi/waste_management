WASTEVISION CLASSIFICATION

Four Categories. One Cleaner Future.

WasteVision organizes waste into four primary categories to support better identification, segregation and management.

01
☣️
CATEGORY 01

Hazardous Waste

Waste that may pose risks to human health or the environment and therefore requires appropriate handling and disposal.

Examples
Medical Waste Chemical Waste Batteries Toxic Materials
⚠️
Handle Carefully

Requires appropriate collection and disposal procedures.

02
🌱
CATEGORY 02

Organic Waste

Biodegradable material that can naturally decompose and may be suitable for composting or biological treatment.

Examples
Food Waste Kitchen Waste Garden Waste Plant Matter
🌿
Compost & Recover

Suitable organic waste can be processed through composting.

03
🗑️
CATEGORY 03

Inorganic Waste

Non-biodegradable waste that generally does not decompose naturally and requires appropriate disposal or recovery.

Examples
Mixed Waste Ceramics Non-Recyclable Items Synthetic Materials
🗑️
Dispose Responsibly

Should be collected and managed through suitable disposal systems.

04
♻️
CATEGORY 04

Recyclable Waste

Materials that can potentially be collected, processed and used again as secondary raw materials.

Examples
Paper Plastic Metal Cardboard
♻️
Recover & Recycle

Keep recyclable materials separate to support material recovery.
