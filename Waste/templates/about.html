🌿 ABOUT WASTEVISION

Smarter Technology. Cleaner Communities.

WHO WE ARE

Rethinking the Way Waste is Managed

WasteVision is a smart waste management platform that brings together Artificial Intelligence, Internet of Things, predictive analytics and Geographic Information Systems.

The platform is designed to support better waste classification, intelligent bin monitoring, predictive planning, optimized collection routes and citizen participation.

Our vision is to create a connected ecosystem where waste-related information can be transformed into meaningful actions.
